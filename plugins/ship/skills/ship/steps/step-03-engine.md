# Delegation is an explicit exception

Default: the lead works solo, including verification. -e and -m solo mean
no workers or reviewers. Never launch an advisor in their place.

Before any other mode, explain the independent units and the expected benefit
over doing them directly. Ask for approval. -a, --auto and --yolo do not
grant it. Without approval or runtime capability, continue solo.

After approval, use the active harness native agent API, at most three bounded
workers, no nested delegation. Do not request bypassPermissions or retune
models. Read references/role-templates.md only when actually delegating.
Do useful non-overlapping work while workers run; collect each result once.
